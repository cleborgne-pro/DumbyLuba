# Version history

The current version is the `versionName` in `app/build.gradle.kts`. Details of recent versions are in the documents under [docs/](docs).

Version 0.3.0: touch zoom/rotation/panning, Lines and Checkerboard modes, outer border and no-go passes (orange). Computation compared across several orientations and directions, with safe links only. STOP in the Debug panel. See `docs/MAP_PREVIEW_TEST.md`.

Version 0.4.0: multi-zone selection (map and chips), computation progress per zone, rounded map with floating controls and a preparation panel. Each zone keeps its own orientation and sections; no invented inter-zone links.

Version 0.4.1: border passes are now taken out of the blue routes and drawn separately, as orange dashes (outline lap and no-go). The perimeter lap is still counted in the estimated meters, but the preview no longer mixes it up with the mowing rows.

Version 0.4.2: the preview is drawn as stripes to the scale of the robot's cutting width (0.40 m by default, adjustable). Since each pass is drawn separately and semi-transparent, overlaps get darker and gaps show up. Borders are drawn the same way in orange, with a thin line for the theoretical path.

Version 0.4.3: palette reworked to stay neutral against the green of the zones. Cutting grid in semi-transparent white, borders in semi-transparent beige, 1 px opaque center line (theoretical path), borders drawn solid instead of dashed. The 1 px center line is opaque white.

Version 0.4.5: the number of passes on the outer border and on no-go outlines is adjustable (1 to 4). The rings are concentric: each additional pass is offset inward by the spacing, so never closer to the limits than the safety margin. The border not touching the outline is explained by this margin: the robot's center stays "margin" away from the limit and the cutting stripe is 0.40 m wide, so an unmowed ring of half-width remains around the outline. Reducing the margin (0.3 m) shrinks it; this is a safety choice, not a drawing defect.

Version 0.4.6: the map takes the full screen height (no more scrolling below the map on the Map tab). Zones, mode, settings and the GENERATE button are grouped in a dialog opened by the floating button. The loading dialog is removed: the route is drawn live on the map as the orientation search progresses (partial previews published by the planner).

Version 0.5.0: UX/UI overhaul inspired by the Mammotion app. A single theme (`ui/theme`) sets the signature green palette, light backgrounds and rounded corners; shared components (`ui/components`) provide cards, stat tiles, status chips and label/value rows. Navigation moves to a bottom bar with four tabs (Home, Map, Robot, Account): the home screen shows the robot's state, a clickable map preview and telemetry as tiles; the diagnostics panel is split into sub-tabs (Robot, Tests, Blades, Logs, Settings); the Account screen takes over the Mammotion login cards. The emergency stop remains reachable without scrolling (dedicated button at the top of the home screen and of the Robot panel), on a red background like the stop in the original app. No diagnostics function is changed: this is a reorganization and a reskin.

Version 0.5.1: dialogs become full width (`LubaDialog` component, reduced margins, scrolling content and actions pinned at the bottom) and checkboxes adopt the brand green through `LubaCheckbox` / `LubaCheckRow` (the whole row is clickable, light green background when the option is on).

Version 0.5.2 to 0.5.5: styling pass — map colors centralized in the theme, spacing grid and `LubaSection` sections, `ResultRow` / `SliderRow` / `EmergencyButton` / `LegendSwatch` components, harmonized STOP, then readability of the UI code (`ConnectionState` and `ShapeKind` enums, single French formatting, `MapScreen` reviewed).

Version 0.6.0: dark theme following the system theme by default, with a System / Light / Dark choice in Debug panel → Settings (choice remembered). Palette, shapes, shared components and map adapt to both themes; label plurals fixed ("1 zone", "3 zones").

Version 0.7.0: supervised random walk in Debug panel → Navigation (real robot, random segments with STOP between each segment, live map with robot and track when available, optional mowing with OFF check and ON confirmation). Bounded `DrvMotionCtrl` command, continuous guards, maximum radius and zone confinement, measurements of displacement and stopping delay to calibrate the raw units.

Version 0.31.0: **adjustable start point** in the route preview. Version 0.33.1: **terrain no longer inverted**. The automatic orientation of the gateway phone is deduced from the thrust of the robot's starts and stops; since the tilt arrives about 1 s later than the position, the thrust fell on the 2nd measurement of the pass and the orientation found was flipped by 180°: blue (low) on high ground, brown (high) in hollows. Each start now compares the first 3 measurements of the pass with the next 3 (and each stop the last 3 with the previous 3); an inconsistent result is no longer kept, and those measurements are excluded from the terrain. An orientation **Saved** by the flat-ground reference while the terrain was inverted remains wrong: choose the phone's actual position, then redo the reference. See [terrain](docs/RELIEF.md#phone-orientation-automatic).

Version 0.34.0: **mowing history and end-of-mowing report**. Every real mowing session (blades running) is measured from launch to finish: duration off and on charge, battery consumed and recharged, recharges, cut distance, obstacles bypassed and resumes, against the estimate frozen at the start (computed with the robot's actual battery). At the end, a **report** opens (with a "Mowing finished" notification): estimated and actual duration and recharges with a verdict (on target within ±10%, X% longer or shorter), observed battery life, recharge speed and duration factor. Service → **Mowing history** lists the last 200 mowing sessions and reopens each report. **Later estimates adapt**: duration factor (pivots, links, returns), battery life and recharge duration observed over the robot's 8 most recent sessions replace the settings once there are enough measurements; a trial mowing session can be excluded. See [mowing history](docs/features/mowing-history.md).

Version 0.34.3: **the simulation leaves the base like the robot**. Every departure from the station (first departure and resume after recharge) starts with the *Leaving base · slow forward* phase: straight line from the base to the link point at 0.1 m/s, without pivoting, like the real exit, then a link at normal speed. This duration is included in the reserve checked before each resume and in the estimates. The simulation also sets off as soon as the return threshold + 10 points is reached, like real mowing, instead of waiting for the resume threshold. The automatic pause in the background is shown in the log, and the base exit, the end and any blockage (with its cause) are written to the diagnostics. See [simulation](docs/features/simulation.md) and [MOWING_SIMULATION](docs/MOWING_SIMULATION.md#leaving-the-base--0343).

Version 0.35.0: **tight obstacle bypass, on the most favorable side**. After a contact (or an obstacle added during the mission), the remaining route is no longer linked through the navigation's comfort ring (≈ 1.2 m from the obstacle's center, first path found): each link first tries a tight detour — tangent, arc and tangent around the obstacle (≈ 1 m from its center) — on the left and on the right, checked by the same controls as the mission (safe area, envelope, pivots, tracking reserve). The side chosen is the cheapest: length and pivots, heavily penalized within 1.5 m of a zone edge, an exclusion or a saved obstacle, hence **the inner side near an edge**. With the blades stopped, the 50 cm rule at edges still takes priority. Bypasses are 5 to 16% shorter on the reference zones. The tracking message and the log (`route_obstacle_bypass` → `detours`) state the side. See [bypass](docs/OBSTACLE_BYPASS.md#tight-detour-and-side-choice-since-0350).

Version 0.36.0: **obstacles encountered on the map and difficulties heatmap**. Every bumper contact during an autonomous move is recorded with the object's estimated position, the bypass side and the photo taken after the back-off; stuck events, **wheel slip** (new detection, relative to the robot's usual progress, without stopping), lifts, lack of progress and route deviations too (`filesDir/difficulties.json`). On the map, two layers: **Obstacles** (contacts, card with photo on tap, **Dismiss** or **Save obstacle**) and **Difficulties** (heatmap). Taken into account in later routes: an object hit twice at the same spot becomes a **presumed obstacle** avoided by the preparation, blades-stopped moves go around difficult areas when the detour stays reasonable (×1.5 + 4 m), and bypasses pick the least difficult side. Settings and list in Service → Obstacles. See [obstacles encountered and difficulties](docs/DIFFICULTIES.md).

Version 0.37.0: **obstacle simulation driven by the real navigation, shorter transit after a contact**. In Service → Obstacle simulation, the simulated robot no longer moves edge by edge with a pivot at each vertex (uncorrected line deviation, erratic headings on finely cut borders): it is driven by the real mowing's tracking module (`PathFollower`, predicted pose, blade ranges, confirmed arrival, 30 cm safety stop) at the set forward speed, on a mechanical model of the LUBA 2 (`SimulatedRobot`, shared with the execution bench). Real mowing and simulation: an obstacle met during a **move without mowing** (link, access, return to base) no longer leads back onto the line just behind it; the whole move is recomputed by the shortest path to its destination (10.0 m instead of 13.7 m on an L-shaped transit; repaired routes never longer on the reference zones). See [bypass](docs/OBSTACLE_BYPASS.md#non-mowing-transit-direct-link-to-the-destination-since-0370).

Version 0.40.0: **recorded outlines = outer side of the robot**. Zone limits and no-go zones recorded by the robot describe the outer edge of the chassis, not its center. All center paths therefore stay **at least half a width (25.7 cm)** from the outline (`RecordedBoundary`):
- outer lap at 25.7 cm plus the set margin;
- no-go laps at 25.7 + 5 cm, compared with 8 cm before, when the robot entered them by 18 cm;
- passes and links never closer.

During a pivot or a tracking deviation, the chassis may overhang the outline by at most 15 cm; this is the area used by the Fold's and the gateway's footprint checks. The "Outer lap inset" setting (25 cm, measured from the center) becomes **"Outer lap margin"**, measured from the robot's side: 5 cm by default, new key, hence a center at 30.7 cm. The survey lap margin becomes 10 cm, and border following also measures the deviation from the robot's side. Coverage is counted on what the blades can reach: a 5.6 cm strip along the outlines, set margins and corners rounded to the pivot radius excluded. The reference zones stay above 95%. Laps impractical for the chassis (no-go too close to the limit, passage narrower than the robot) are skipped instead of refusing the whole computation. A robot stopped too close to an edge to pivot first backs out straight along its axis. A route segment kept to the end finishes exactly on the base's approach point.

Version 0.40.1: **outer lap margin applied to no-go zones and passes** (bumper contacts at the border, Oct 14). A no-go zone's lap stayed half a width + 5 cm from its outline whatever the set margin (8 cm from the center before 0.40.0): it now follows the **outer lap margin**, like the perimeter lap, including where the perimeter runs along a no-go zone. Passes stay behind this lap (10 cm from the loop): the bumper stays at least at the no-go zone's set margin at the end of a pass. Without a lap (none requested or lap abandoned), passes ignored the margin and stopped at the pivot radius: they now stop at the lap distance + 20 cm, as behind a lap. Two no-go zones closer than twice this distance are bypassed by a single lap (an isolated lap passed less than half a width from its neighbor). The area to cover follows these strips. Pass cutting falls back to JTS's robust overlay when the classic one fails on the rotated area (Enfer at 1 m). With the app's default margin (5 cm), plans are identical to those of 0.40.0. Tests: `NoGoOffsetTest` (including two neighboring no-go zones), `CoveragePlannerTest`; `RoutePlanningTest` (Fosse) moves to the app's default margin, and the Fosse chimney (`OptimizedModeTest`) is detected down to y = −1.5 m.

Version 0.40.2: **resuming a mowing session interrupted after a contact possible again** (Oct 14, 17:31). Version 0.41.0: **detailed mowing bar, pivots on slopes tolerated, passes stopped in the middle of the lap** (mowing on Oct 15, Jardin d'Eden).
- **Mowing bar**: "Mowed x%" measures the meters of cutting stripes over the whole mission, excluding moves, and no longer drops back to 0 after a bypass or a resume (it used to count route edges). The bar also shows the blade rotation reported by the robot (rpm or "stopped"), the reported height (with the setpoint during an adjustment), the battery, the actual / estimated duration and a progress bar. The "Mowing in progress" page shows the same information.
- **Pivot on a slope**: at the bottom of the Jardin d'Eden, each U-turn pivot made the robot slide 20 to 45 cm downhill, hence 12 "Route deviation >30 cm" stops in 10 minutes. During a pivot and the reconvergence that follows it (until back within 15 cm of the line, 2 m at most), the tolerated deviation becomes 60 cm, with the chassis inside the safe area and outside the base zone (`PivotSlide`, same rule on the Fold, the gateway and the simulation). Beyond that, the "Slide…" stop is recovered by a back-off then a resume.
- **Pass ends**: with an outer lap, passes stop half a cut inside its track, including in the corners and lobes the lap rounds off. Pockets recovered behind no-go zones did not follow this rounding: some passes went to within 1 cm of the lap, or even beyond (`ZigzagBehindLoopTest`).
